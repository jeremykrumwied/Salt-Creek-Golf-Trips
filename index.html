Food & Beverage HQ: Room 210

Room 210 is stocked as the central hub for snacks and light meals. Options available in 210 include chips, lunch meat, bread, frozen breakfast sandwiches, and muffins. Grab whatever you'd like, or bring your own food!

Meal Options:

  • Breakfast: Room 210 options or local spots (McDonald's, Brown County Inn, Ooey Gooey).
  • Lunch: Sandwiches in 210 or lunch at the 19th Hole Bar.
  • Dinner: 19th Hole Bar on course (Friday & Saturday).
Pro-Tip for Beverages: Make sure to bring your own drinks/beverages for hanging out after the 19th Hole Bar closes!
